Buying your Linked Helper licence

Your free trial ends two weeks after your first campaign starts. Here's which licence to buy, the two discounts, and exactly where to click.

These pictures were taken on 8 October 2026. If what you're looking at doesn't match, go by the words rather than the picture - or just ask me.

Your Linked Helper trial lasts 14 days, and the clock only starts when your first campaign sends. When it runs out, your campaign stops until there's a licence on your account. Buying one takes about five minutes.

Which one to get

Standard, for 12 months. Buying a year at a time takes 45% off, and Standard is plenty for the pace your one campaign runs at. Then my promo code takes a further 10% off your first purchase.

Step by step

  1. Go to linkedhelper.com/member/accounts and sign in with your Linked Helper email and password - the account you made when you installed it, not your LinkedIn login.
  2. Click the blue Buy license button, top right of the LinkedIn accounts page.

Do not press Proceed to Payment until you have entered your promo code at the bottom of this form.

  1. Under License type, choose Standard.
  2. Under Plans, choose 12 months - the one marked -45%. The bottom of the window should now say 12 months Standard license.
  1. Check Billing country further down says where you are.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the same window. Under Promo code, paste my code and click Apply. Copy it exactly - the two dashes at the end are part of it: LHU-BFXSJTJQUUHJGSPSODJDAQ--
  1. Only now, click Proceed to Payment - once the price shows the 10% off - and pay as you normally would.

After you've paid

There's nothing else to do. Buy it any time before your trial runs out - Linked Helper holds the new licence and switches it on by itself the moment the trial ends. Your 12 months only start counting from then, so buying early costs you nothing.

Don't activate it yourself. Leave it to switch over on its own.

Why Standard, and the one catch

Standard does everything your one campaign needs. Pro mainly lifts a few daily limits that matter if you run several kinds of campaign at once.

The catch: a Standard licence can't be switched to Pro part-way through. It can only be changed once it runs out. So if you already know you'll be reaching out to a big existing list - your past clients, say - talk to me before you buy.
