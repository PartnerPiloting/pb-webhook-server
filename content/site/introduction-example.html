What an introduction looks like

One line in a chat, and this is the draft waiting in your mailbox.

You tell Wingguy who to introduce and why - something like "introduce Priya to Mark, they're both in the inner circle". It pulls what it knows about each of them and writes the email. You read it, change anything you like, and press send.

Introducing Priya Nair and Mark Delaney

Hi Priya / Hi Mark,

Mark - Priya and I have been working together for a while now, and she's someone I'd like you to know.

Priya Nair is Melbourne-based and the founder of Sitewise Training - short, practical safety courses for construction crews.
She's just signed her first national builder, rolling the courses out across forty sites.
Her whole approach is training people on the job, not in a classroom, and it's catching on fast.
Priya - Mark Delaney is in Newcastle and works as a fractional CFO for growing construction and trade businesses.
He's a regular at industry events, and one of the most generous connectors I know.
He's put several exceptional people my way.
Here's why I think you two should connect: it's not so much a functional fit - it's that you're both in the inner circle, and you're both good people. We're all about meeting and recommending great people to one another, and I believe you'll be brilliant at recommending each other - but that only works if each of you knows what the other offers. That way, when the right person crosses your path, you'll recognise them straight away.

Priya, could you please reach out to Mark at mark@example.com to arrange a quick Zoom?

Really looking forward to hearing how this one unfolds!

The names and details are made up; the layout is the real one. Each person gets their own box, each name links to their LinkedIn profile, and the blue box says why they should talk.

What to notice

It's two-sided. Each person learns who the other is and why they matter - not just "meet my friend".

One person makes the first move. Usually the one who'll get the most from it - and the other person's email address is right there in the body, so nobody has to go hunting.

It doesn't have to be a business fit. This one isn't. It's two good people who'll be brilliant at recommending each other once they know what the other offers - which is how the inner circle works.
