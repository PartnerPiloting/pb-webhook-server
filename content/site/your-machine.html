Choosing the machine

This is the first screen after you make an account. Almost everything on it is already set the way you want it - the location, Ubuntu, the newest LTS version.

One thing is not. Look at section 2. The row selected by default is the small one at $4.90. You need the 2 VCPUs / 4 GB / 60 GB row at $19.60, and it will not select itself. It's the only place on these screens where doing nothing gives you the wrong answer.

Adding my key

In section 3, click View All. That opens the fields Binary Lane keeps folded away, and one of them saves us both a job: SSH Keys, with a + Add SSH Key button.

Put my key in there and I can get straight into your machine and build it, without you having to send me anything private. Name it Wingguy, paste this into the Public Key box exactly as it is, tick the box to use it for future machines, and click Create:

The one number to send me

After you pay, a box appears saying Your server is ready!. Send me the Primary Ip - four numbers with dots between them. That's the only thing I need.

It isn't a secret, so text it, Zoom chat it, email it, whatever's easiest. Your key is already on the machine, so I can get in and start building straight away.

Then it's mine

I build the whole thing on my own from there - the desktop, Linked Helper, the self-restart, the nightly backup, the monitoring. You don't install anything, and nothing goes on your laptop. Then one short call where you sign in to LinkedIn on it, and that's the only part that needs you.
