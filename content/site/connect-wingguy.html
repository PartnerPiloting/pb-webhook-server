Connecting Wingguy to your Claude

One link, added once in your Claude settings. After that Wingguy is simply there in every chat, and you talk to it in plain English. About five minutes.

We normally do this together on your first call, so if that's coming up, you can leave it for then. This page is for doing it on your own - or doing it again on another computer.

What you're doing, in plain terms

Claude is the AI chat you already use - the one at claude.ai, or the Claude app on your computer. On its own it knows nothing about you. A connector is how you plug something into it. Add the Wingguy connector once, and your Claude can see your leads, your conversations, your calendar and your setup - and can answer "where are we up to?" instead of guessing.

Nothing is sent anywhere by this. Wingguy still never sends a message or an email without you pressing the button.

Your link

Your Wingguy portal shows the link, under Setup and help, in the box called Connect Wingguy to your Claude. Press Copy link. That's the whole thing you'll paste in a moment.

That link is yours alone - it opens your account and nobody else's. Don't forward it, and don't paste it into a shared document.

Step by step

  1. Open Claude - claude.ai in your browser, or the Claude app - and sign in the way you normally do.
  2. Find Settings. Click your initials or name, bottom-left on a computer, and choose Settings.
  3. Open Connectors. It's one of the headings down the left of Settings. If you can't see it, tell me - a couple of Claude plans hide it.
  4. Click Add custom connector. It asks for a name and a URL. Name it Wingguy, paste the link you copied into the URL box, and click Add. Leave everything else as it is.
  5. Start a new chat and type "what can I do with Wingguy?" If it answers like it knows you, you're done. Back in your portal, the Connect box will say Connected and when your Claude last used it.

If the chat answers like it has never heard of Wingguy

Usually the connector was added but not switched on for that chat. In the chat box, click the little + or tools button, find Wingguy in the list, and make sure it's ticked. Then ask again.

If the portal never says Connected

The link in Claude isn't yours. It happens when a link travels by email or gets copied from someone else. Delete the Wingguy connector in Claude, copy the link from your own portal again, and add it fresh.

On your phone, or another computer

Connectors belong to your Claude account, not the device, so once it's added it's there everywhere you sign in. Nothing to repeat.

What to say first

Two phrases do most of the work from here. "Where are we up to?" tells you where you are in getting set up. "Continue" takes the next step. Everything else is just asking, in your own words.
