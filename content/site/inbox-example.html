Your inbox, one thing at a time

Say "go through my inbox" to Claude - or "help me with my emails", or whatever springs to mind. Wingguy reads every email, clears the ones that need nothing from you, and takes you through the rest one by one.

Do this first - two minutes

Claude has its own email connection, and it reaches for that first. It doesn't know your leads, your clients or your calendar, so none of what's below happens. One line in your Claude settings fixes it for good.

The line goes into Claude's memory list - a list of short notes Claude reads before every chat. Claude's Settings look slightly different depending on your account, so there are two ways in. Both end at the same list.

  1. In Claude, click your name at the bottom left, then Settings.
  2. Look at the menu down the left-hand side of Settings:
    • If you see Your Claude: click it, scroll down to the heading Memory, and click the Manage button next to "Your memory".
    • If you see Memory instead: click it.
  1. You'll now see a list of notes (yours may be empty), with a typing box underneath. Click in that box, type this line exactly, and press Enter: Whenever I ask about my inbox or my emails, use Wingguy, not Gmail or Outlook.
  1. Check the line now shows in the list. Then close Settings.
  2. Start a new chat - a chat you already had open won't know about the line - and type "go through my inbox".
  3. The first time, Claude asks before using wingguy inbox. Click Always allow, so it never needs to ask again.

How to tell it worked: under your message Claude shows "Used Wingguy", then counts your emails. If it shows "Used Gmail" or "Used Outlook" instead, the line isn't in your memory list yet - go back to step 1. And if the Manage button is greyed out, switch on "Generate memory from chats" just above it first.

If it ever asks again after I've improved something, click Always allow again.

Why it's different

The inbox tools you see advertised only know your emails. Wingguy knows your leads, your clients and your calendar, so it can:

  • tell you "this is the lead you met on Thursday", not just "an email from Sarah"
  • check whether something is already done before it asks you to do it
  • draft the reply in your own voice

What it will never do

  • Delete anything. Clearing an email just takes it out of your inbox - it's still in your mailbox.
  • Send anything. Replies go into your Drafts, and you press send.
  • Clear anything without your yes. "Later" leaves an email exactly where it is.
  • Clear a personal email unanswered. If a person wrote to you, it offers a reply first and lets you decide.

Tell it how you like things

Say it once - "always clear LinkedIn notifications", "anything from my accountant is always a job" - and it remembers next time.

If you have a big backlog, it works through 50 at a time and tells you how many are left. On Outlook it reads and sorts your inbox now, and clearing is coming shortly.
