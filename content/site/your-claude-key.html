Setting up your Claude key

The drafting inside LinkedIn and your overnight follow-ups run on a key that's yours, under a spend limit you choose. About fifteen minutes, most of it Anthropic's website.

Anthropic rearranges its website now and then. If a button isn't quite where I say, go by the words - or just ask me.

What it's for

Two parts of Wingguy work for you away from your chat. Overnight, it goes through your follow-ups and gets your replies ready. And when you type /wg inside LinkedIn, the drafting happens on my servers. Both run on a Claude key that's yours, not mine.

This is not the Claude subscription you already pay for. Same company, possibly the same email, but a separate account with separate billing. Your subscription powers your chat. This key powers the drafting inside LinkedIn and your overnight follow-ups. It's usually a few dollars a month.

And the reason you can relax about it: you put a monthly spend limit on it, a number you choose, and you can switch it off any time. The worst case in the whole world is a bill the size of the limit you set.

Step by step

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign in, or create the account if it's your first visit. It's a different login from claude.ai, even if it's the same email - and the site now calls itself Claude Platform, so don't worry when that's what you see.
  2. Add a little credit. Billing, in the left-hand menu, then add a payment method and a small starting credit. US$5 is plenty. This is the part people trip on - your subscription doesn't cover it. It's pay-as-you-go.
  3. Create a workspace called Wingguy. A workspace is just a walled-off compartment. It matters because of the next step.
  4. Set a monthly spend limit on that workspace - say US$20. Because it sits on the workspace rather than your whole account, it's a hard ceiling on what Wingguy can ever cost you, and it can't touch anything else you use the account for.

Don't skip the spend limit. It's the step that makes the whole thing safe.

  1. Create the key inside that workspace. API keys, then Create key, and name it Wingguy. Copy it the moment it appears - it's shown once and never again. If you miss it, no drama. Delete it and make another.
  2. Paste it into your Wingguy portal. Open Settings, then Your Claude Key. If you can't see Settings, open My Wingguy and you'll find the same box on your setup page. Paste, then Save key. It checks the key with Anthropic before saving, so you'll know straight away whether it's good.

Treat it like a password

Don't email it to me or paste it anywhere else. It only ever needs to go into your own portal.

If it says the key didn't work

The usual reason is step 2 - a real key on an account with no credit yet. Add the credit, then paste the same key again.

If your card is declined at step 2

It's almost always your bank, not Anthropic. The charge is in US dollars, and Australian banks often block the first overseas online payment. Check your banking app for a blocked payment to approve, or try a different card. Choosing Card rather than Google Pay or Apple Pay lets you pick exactly which card it uses.

If you change your mind

You can revoke the key from the console in about ten seconds, and everything on my side simply stops. You're never locked in, and you can see exactly what it's spent any time you like. If you ever revoke it and want to start again, make a fresh key and paste it into the same box.
