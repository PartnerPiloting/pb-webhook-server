# You don't make 'em - you find 'em

Some of the people you meet will want what you have and can afford it. Most won't. That is not networking failing - that is networking doing its job.

There is no point trying to turn the wrong person into the right person. You can't make 'em. You can only find 'em.

So relax. When you reach out directly, the right ones will always be the minority. Which is exactly why it pays to have a lot of other good guys who understand what you offer and are setting up Zooms for you with people you would never have found yourself.

But they can only scout for you if they can say what you do. There is no point being super clear about your value in your own head if no one else is. Make it so simple - who you help, and what changes for them - that when a good guy hears someone describe that problem, your name comes to mind on the spot.

And it works both ways. Be determined to be a good scout for them.

**One thing for this week:** pick one good guy you already know and ask them, "If you were describing what I do to someone, what would you say?" If the answer is fuzzy, that is your job to fix, not theirs. Then ask who they are looking for, and go find one.
