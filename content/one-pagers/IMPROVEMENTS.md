# Improvements waiting - the series and the weekly client emails

Internal. Never published (listed in `NON_PIECE_FILES` in services/onePagerContent.js).

Every improvement Guy has to an email lands here the moment he says it - in chat, on a call, in
a voice note - with the date and his words. Whoever next revises a piece reads its section first
and works the waiting items in; Guy sees the new text before it is committed. Then each item is
closed: **Done** (date, commit) or **Dropped** (date, why). Nothing is deleted.

How to file an item:

- Put it under the piece it improves (by slug). An idea for an email that does not exist yet
  goes under **New pieces**.
- Keep Guy's words. Add a note on placement or per-client handling if it was discussed.
- Status is one of: **Waiting**, **Done**, **Dropped**.

What does NOT go here (see docs/CLIENT-TOUCH-PLANNER.md, "Where your input goes"):
something about one client (their note or a pin), a rule about how the weekly emails work (the
design doc), and business ideas unrelated to the series (Guy's ideas.md).

### staying-on-top-of-today - DONE as make-the-important-urgent ("Make the important urgent")

- *20 Oct 2026, Guy:* 100 connection requests a week (LinkedIn's ceiling) at a good acceptance
  rate = dozens of new people a week to thank in the Today tab. By hand - look at their posts and
  profile, craft a reply - that is 10+ minutes each and untenable. With Wingguy it is about 30
  seconds, so it becomes feasible: Wingguy is your best friend and you keep improving it. Not
  overwhelming - get to it every day and keep it clear, then clear the follow-ups the same way,
  and you're on top of it, feeling great, and getting discovery calls. What breaks it: the
  campaign runs out of people on the Linked Helper machine (how many thank-yous do you want to
  do? Expect to do the numbers, not one thank-you = one call). And for those who chose Reconnect:
  people from past conversations turn up too - "enormous gold" - so 20 a day of those is often
  worth it, unless you've changed direction so the past messaging no longer fits. **Done** (20 Oct, approved - make-the-important-urgent.md)
  - Acceptance rate - do NOT quote "this week's accepted / this week's sent" (Rick 38/100, Sam
    36/97, Roland 16/78 to 18 Oct): Guy, 20 Oct - "the rate creeps up because it takes time for
    people to respond", so a week's figure understates, most of all for a new campaign. The
    honest number is a MATURED group: of people invited 2-5 weeks ago, how many have accepted -
    the Linked Helper watchdog already records this (cohort_invited / cohort_accepted, see the
    Monday campaign numbers). In the body: no fixed percentage; say acceptances build over the
    first couple of weeks. The planner drops in the client's own matured rate once their
    campaign is old enough to have one. Guy's 100-a-week arithmetic stays as the example.
  - Reconnect paragraph = per-client extra (only clients who chose it - Mehnaaz declined).
  - Overlaps: twenty-second-thank-you (the thank-you itself), four-hours-not-forty (why it is now
    feasible), habit email #1 "A few minutes a morning" (the daily nudge). This piece is the
    numbers + the routine + what breaks it; link, don't repeat.
  - Eligible only once the client's Today tab has records. Planner event: "campaign running low
    on people" -> this piece (or its section) + a flag to Guy.

- *20 Oct 2026, Guy - the heart of the piece:* "This is where most people fall down." Guy's
  long-held principle: **make the proactive reactive.** Networking is important but never urgent,
  so it loses to everything urgent. Putting it in their inbox - the Today tab - makes the
  important urgent: something waiting for you that you deal with. And "I don't have time" stops
  holding up, because each one takes so little time and /wg is a pleasure to use - "the people

  **Done** (20 Oct, approved - make-the-important-urgent.md)

## Pool-model caveat - endings that say "that's next"

- *20 Oct 2026:* Several client endings tease the following piece ("that's next"). In a fixed
  sequence that is fine; in the weekly planner's pool, a piece can be picked out of order and the
  tease becomes false. The opening run keeps first-discovery-call -> connection-isnt-charm ->
  discovery-call-craft -> reason-to-follow-up together for this reason. Before the planner picks
  freely, every client ending needs checking: rewrite teases as hand-ons that stay true whatever
  comes next, or the planner must keep tease pairs together. **Waiting**
