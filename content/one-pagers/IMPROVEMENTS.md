# Improvements waiting - the series and the weekly client emails

Internal. Never published (listed in `NON_PIECE_FILES` in services/onePagerContent.js).

Every improvement Guy has to an email lands here the moment he says it - in chat, on a call, in
a voice note - with the date and his words. Whoever next revises a piece reads its section first
and works the waiting items in; Guy sees the new text before it is committed. Then each item is
closed: **Done** (date, commit) or **Dropped** (date, why). Nothing is deleted.

How to file an item:

- Put it under the piece it improves (by slug). An idea for an email that does not exist yet
  goes under **New pieces**.
- Keep Guy's words. Add a note on placement or per-client handling if it was discussed.
- Status is one of: **Waiting**, **Done**, **Dropped**.

What does NOT go here (see docs/CLIENT-TOUCH-PLANNER.md, "Where your input goes"):
something about one client (their note or a pin), a rule about how the weekly emails work (the
design doc), and business ideas unrelated to the series (Guy's ideas.md).

### energy - "What energy do you bring to the table?" (opening run #2, first MINDSET piece)

- *20 Oct 2026, Guy:* What energy do you bring, how important is it, and do you have to be a
  certain personality? Agreed angle: the mirror of score-on-attitude (they are choosing you on
  yours); energy is not volume or being an extrovert - it is what people feel after talking to
  you; the answer to "a certain personality?" is no, but you choose the energy you bring, every

  connection-isnt-charm was. Sent as its own email, never linked. **Waiting**

### busy-weeks - short piece on keeping going when you are flat out

- *20 Oct 2026, Guy:* "What if I've got a lot of work on? Should I keep the habit going - or,
  more importantly, keep working to make sure I form it?" Answer: don't stop, drop to the
  minimum; early on, forming the habit matters more than volume. No fixed slot - the planner
  sends it when a client goes quiet or Guy notes they are slammed. **Waiting**

## Existing pieces

### discovery-call-craft - "What to say in the discovery call"

- *20 Oct 2026, Guy:* Book the first call for half an hour with another real call straight
  after it. About ten minutes before the end: "Look, I've got another call in ten minutes. Let
  me give you a quick overview of what I'm about - but would you be up for booking another time
  to talk properly?" Book call two before hanging up. The piece says the first call earns the
  second but not how to book it - this is the how. The other call must be real, never a made-up
  excuse. **Waiting**
- *20 Oct 2026, Guy:* The booking line: "How does the same time next week suit you? See if
  you've got a spare slot then." The mechanics (calendar open, duplicate today's invite, move it
  a week, practise before the first call) go in only for clients whose note says they need the
  how-to - not for experienced salespeople. **Waiting**
