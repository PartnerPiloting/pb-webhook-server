# Make the important urgent

**Why networking always loses**

Networking is important. It's almost never urgent. And in a busy week, urgent wins every time - the client email, the deadline, the phone call. So the important thing waits, and waits, until it quietly stops.

That's why your Today tab exists. It **makes the important urgent.** Every morning there's something waiting for you - people to thank for connecting, people to follow up - like mail in your inbox. You don't have to remember to network. You just clear what's there.

**Why "I don't have time" stops being true**

Do the numbers. A campaign sends up to 100 invitations a week. Acceptances build over the first couple of weeks, and before long you've got dozens of new people a week to thank.

By hand, that's impossible. Look at their profile, read a post, work out something worth saying - ten minutes each, easily. Nobody keeps that up.

With /wg it's about thirty seconds. It reads them, drafts something specific, and you tweak and send. Most people using it say they enjoy it - the time flies - and that the result beats what they'd have dashed off themselves on a busy day.

**The routine**

- **Open Today with your first coffee.** A few minutes, most days.
- **Thank everyone who's connected.**
- **Then clear your follow-ups** the same way.
- **Done.** You're on top of it - and on top of it is where the discovery calls come from.

**Start right, or it becomes a chore**

This is the part people get wrong. Clear it from the very first week, and it stays a few pleasant minutes a day. Let it build for a fortnight, and it becomes a pile you dread - and a pile you dread doesn't get done.

**What can slow it down**

- **Your campaign runs out of people.** When the thank-yous dry up, it's usually the search, not you - tell me and we'll top it up. And expect to do the numbers: it's rarely one thank-you, one call.

**One thing for this week:** open your Today tab tomorrow morning and clear it - every box, top to bottom.
