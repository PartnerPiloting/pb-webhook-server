# What energy do you bring?

**They're choosing too**

You choose who belongs in your network on attitude, not job title. Here's the other side of that: every person you meet is quietly doing the same to you. Half an hour in, they're asking themselves one question - *do I want more of this person?*

Your answer to that question is the energy you bring.

**Energy isn't volume**

When people hear "energy" they picture the loud one at the networking breakfast - quick jokes, firm handshake, working the room. If that's not you, relax. That isn't what people remember.

What they remember is how they *felt* talking to you. Listened to. Interested in. A bit lighter than when they came in. Quiet people do that brilliantly. Loud people who talk about themselves do the opposite.

**What it looks like on a call**

I went back through a batch of my own first calls to see what actually made people open up. It was never the clever stuff. It was this:

- **Start with them, not the business.** Where they are, something in their background, something about your own week.
- **Hand them the floor - and say so.** "What matters is you" - and then let them talk.
- **Follow what they just said.** Your next question picks up their last sentence, not your list.
- **Give before you're asked.** "I was thinking of someone as you spoke." No strings, nothing wanted back.
- **Admit your own mess.** A bit of honest imperfection gives them permission to be human too.
- **Mean the praise.** Specific and true - "that's a really sharp way to put it" - or none at all.
- **Put their life first.** Kid's playgroup, dog at the door - "that's important, go for it."

On one call I barely spoke for twenty minutes. Just "yeah" and the odd question, while she talked about a hard year - a redundancy, young kids, a move away from her friends. When I finally shared a story of my own, it matched hers. She booked a second call before we'd finished.

Another person told me halfway through she couldn't afford what I do. I didn't push - I'd just got a good feeling about her, and said so. Ten minutes later she was offering to introduce people to *me*. Generosity tends to meet itself.

**Where it drains away**

The energy goes the moment you start presenting - talking at length about yourself and what you do. I've watched it happen on my own calls. That's one more reason call one is about them: it's not just good manners, it's where the energy lives.

**Do you need a certain personality?**

No. But you do choose the energy you bring - every call, on purpose. Curious, generous, unhurried. Anyone can choose that.

If you're wondering how to actually do it in the first two minutes, that's [Connection isn't charm](/series/connection-isnt-charm).

**One thing for this week:** before your next call, decide how you want them to feel when it ends. Then go and make that happen.
