# What's your network for?

**Picture it a year from now**

Before you choose a single person, picture your network twelve months from today. Not how big it is - what it's *doing* for you.

- **Sending you clients directly?**
- **Full of referral partners** - the accountant, the banker, the coach who already talks to the people you serve?
- **Opening doors into one sector** you want to own?

Each answer leads to different people. Pick yours first, and the choosing gets easy.

**What "better conversations" really means**

You want calls with the right people. But the right person isn't necessarily a better client. It's someone **more willing to recommend you** - someone who is already **meeting the clients you want to meet**. One of those can put you in front of more of the right people than fifty direct approaches.

**Look past the job title**

The person you're reaching out to isn't just a role that might buy what you do. They have things they want to achieve - a promotion, a project to land, a problem their boss keeps raising. Help them with *that*, as a person, and they may well be the one who brings you into the organisation.

**Fewer, better**

A hundred well-chosen people beat a thousand random ones. Each well-chosen person brings their own trusted circle with them. A random one brings nothing but a name in your list.

**One thing for this week:** hit reply and tell me, in a sentence, what you want your network doing for you a year from now. I'll use it to sharpen your search and your scoring.
