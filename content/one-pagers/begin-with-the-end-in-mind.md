# Begin with the end in mind

**What the calls are for**

- **Find out about them.** What they do, who they help, what's hard right now.
- **Ask yourself one question while you listen:** who do I know that they should meet?
- **Connect before you ask anything.** One real, human moment first - people who feel you're human answer properly.

Every new person you get to know is someone else you can refer people to. That's what being a connector is.

**Your turn comes in call two - so book it on call one**

Book each first call for half an hour, with something else straight after it. Spend it on them.

About ten minutes before the end:

> *"Look, I've got another call in ten minutes. Let me give you a quick overview of what I'm about - but would you be up for booking another time to talk properly?"*

Book it before you hang up - "How does the same time next week suit you?" is usually all it takes.

By the second call you know what they're looking for, so you can talk about what you do in a way that lets them recommend you, or see for themselves that they need you.

**How many a week? You pick.**

Choose a number you can keep up every week - not your best week, your ordinary one. Two you do every week beats five you manage once. When you reply, tell me your number.

**Then work backwards**

Is everything you're doing - your messages, your outreach, your profile - getting you in front of enough of the right people for that number? If the calls aren't coming, that's where we look.

**One thing for this week:** pick your number, and when your first call gets booked, book something straight after it.
