# Who do you want in your corner next year?

With the end of the year coming up, it's a good time to step back and think about the next couple of years - not just the next quarter.

Whatever's ahead for you - more clients, fewer hours, a new venture, or something you haven't told anyone yet - one question is worth sitting with:

**Who do you want in your corner for it?**

Most of the good things that come our way arrive through people. A client someone put us onto, an introduction, a word in the right ear. And the people who do that for us are the ones who know us and trust us.

That's what we're building - a growing group of "good Guys" who recommend each other. It helps with whatever you end up doing, because the right people are useful for any endeavour.

**So when the break comes, maybe jot down two things:**

- what you'd like the next couple of years to look like
- the kind of people you'd want around you to get there

I'd love to hear what you come up with when we next talk.
