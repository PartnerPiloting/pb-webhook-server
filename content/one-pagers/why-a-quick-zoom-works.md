# Why "a quick Zoom" works

A small thing I've noticed from a lot of LinkedIn replies. The message that gets someone onto a Zoom isn't the clever one. It's the one that shows you actually looked at them.

**Three things do the work**

- **One genuine compliment about something specific** - a post they wrote, or how they describe their work. Not their job title. "Your post on founders not seeing a problem until someone else points it out caught my eye" beats "Impressive background".
- **Keen to hear more** - curiosity about them, not information about you.
- **A low-key ask** - "Worth a quick Zoom in the next couple of weeks?" Easy to say yes to, and just as easy to say no.

**Why it builds trust**

Nothing in it asks them to buy anything. You've noticed something real about them and you'd like to know more. That's how a conversation between two people starts, and people can tell.

**The bit you don't have to remember**

Wingguy already drafts your replies this way. It reads their posts and profile, picks the one thing worth mentioning, and leaves the pitch out. Plain AI would need you to paste all that in every time. And each time you tweak a draft, Wingguy gets a little closer to how you'd put it.

**One thing for this week:** when a reply comes in, let Wingguy have the first go and see how it reads.
