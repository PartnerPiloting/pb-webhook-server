# Had the edits nag lately?

**What the nag is**

Every time Wingguy writes a LinkedIn message or email for you and you change it before you send it, Wingguy keeps both versions side by side - what it wrote, and what you actually sent.

Those changes are gold. They show how you really like things said. But until someone looks at them, Wingguy can't learn from them. That's what the "unreviewed edits" nag is telling you.

**What I did about mine**

For weeks Wingguy kept nagging me that I had over 100 of them. I kept putting it off. Then one day, sitting by a pool in Chiang Mai, when the nag popped up again, I just said to Claude: "You review them and tell me what you come up with."

It did a brilliant job. It went through all 100, ignored the one-off changes, and picked up habits I didn't even know I had. I always cut a second compliment, I never tell people I'm "fully booked", and I'd rather end on "looking forward to..." than "no rush". Then it turned each of those into an instruction, so my drafts now start out sounding much more like me.

**Why plain Claude couldn't have done it**

Plain Claude never sees what it drafted for you versus what you actually sent, and it forgets everything between chats. Wingguy is what kept all 100 before-and-afters, and Wingguy is where the new instructions now live, so every draft uses them - in Claude, in the portal and in the extension.

Claude did the thinking. Wingguy gave it something worth thinking about, and somewhere to keep the answer.

**You don't have to do it yourself**

We all want our messages to sound like us. But are we ever going to sit down and go through a hundred edits? Probably not. You don't have to. Trust Claude and Wingguy with it, and you can still change or undo anything it saves.

**One thing for this week:** next time you see the nag, type this into Claude:

> review my edits - have a stab at the patterns and save them
