  1. Create the Clients row (Status Paused)
  2. Send the acknowledgement email
  3. Build their leads base
  4. Validate the base against the schema
  5. Finish the row - the defaults below
  6. Mint their Portal Token
  7. Activate - access never waits on my inbox
  8. Create their 20 tasks
  9. Draft the welcome email - never sent
  10. Email me that it's done

Every trap here actually happened.

Provisioning writes Timezone: Australia/Brisbane flat. It is not derived from anything the client typed.

Every meeting time Wingguy ever shows them or their leads uses this field. Wrong timezone = every offered time is wrong, and it looks like a booking bug rather than a field.

Check it on every new joiner. Kay is Maleny, same clock as Brisbane, so she happens to be right - the next one might not be.

Provisioning sets Wingguy Enabled = Yes and Followup Brief = Yes on every joiner. The manual checklist says to leave both off until later.

Wingguy Enabled

It's the extension gate. Harmless if they're getting the extension. For a chat-only client it should be off - turn it off rather than leaving it.

Followup Brief

The old warning here is out of date. It used to say the brief would quietly fall back to my platform key and I'd pay for their nightly run. That is no longer what happens.

The resolver now has three lanes: stored client key, then platform (owner and managed-plan clients only), then none-blocked. A BYO client with no key on the row lands in none-blocked - the brief does not run at all, and it is never billed to me.

So the risk flipped. It's no longer my money; it's their brief silently doing nothing between joining and step 11. Not urgent, but don't promise them an overnight brief before their key is in.

Bases used to need three hand-built fields after every join (Profile Key formula, Date Created, Posts Relevance Status). The zero-UI schema landed on main 4 Sep and removed them.

Proven on staging, not yet on a real prod join.

On Kay's run: the notify email lists anything the base builder couldn't do as MANUAL: lines. Empty means the zero-UI schema held. Anything listed, do it by hand and tell me.

If the payer already has a Clients row (matched on Stripe customer id, then email), provisioning stops before creating anything. It captures the subscription id onto the existing row and emails me.

Deliberate: it may be a rejoin or a duplicate payment. Money is untouched - refunds are mine to do in Stripe.

Nothing is prevented before payment - there's no email pre-check by design. So a duplicate payer pays first and gets sorted after.

The chain stops clean at the failed step, emails me, and keeps its place. Every step is idempotent, so nothing double-runs on resume.

  • Retry: POST /api/join-provision/jobs/:id/retry with the debug key.
  • Ledger is Postgres join_provision_jobs, keyed by checkout session id.
  • Last resort: resend the checkout event from the Stripe dashboard.
